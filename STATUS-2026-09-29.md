**What happened**

- BTC pulled back ~3-4% off recent highs.
- 09-22 emission (all 5 LONG @ $86,620), 09-23 (all 5 LONG @ $86,208),
  09-24 (all 5 LONG @ $86,172) — three consecutive full-portfolio
  entries at the local top.
- Alpha, Charlie, Delta have tight ~4% stops — all three stopped on
  every entry once BTC touched $82.5k on 09-28.
- Bravo, Echo have wider 7-8% stops — still open but underwater ~3%.

**Two structural questions before live capital**

1. **Position stacking**. The shadow model treats every emission as a
   new position. Real executor (Vega's) may or may not stack — need
   to confirm. If it does stack (5 bots × 3 active days = 15 layers),
   deep pullbacks compound. If it caps at one-open-per-bot, week 1
   would look like 5 losing trades instead of 9.

2. **Tight-SL trio getting whipsawed**. Alpha (4.05% SL), Charlie
   (3.93%), Delta (4.01%) all stopped 3× in a week. BTC daily range
   has been running 3-5% — a bar's normal wick is enough to trip them.
   The genome evolution didn't seem to price in this volatility regime.

**Read before deciding on mid-October cohort slot.** One losing week
isn't a kill signal — the SHIP_README expected 40-50% WR real-world,
so we should see recovery weeks. But the STRUCTURAL issue (do bots
stack, are SLs too tight) matters more than the P&L number.
