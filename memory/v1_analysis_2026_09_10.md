## Strategy interpretation

The GA converged on a **momentum-scalper with tight-stop / big-target asymmetry**:

- Watch for any short-term momentum + RSI signal
- Go 100% in when it fires
- Cut fast at −2%, let winners run to +100% or 5 days
- Ignore trend-following almost entirely (weight 0.13)
- Vol scale of 2 means position gets crushed in high-vol regimes → survival mechanism

The near-total rejection of trend-following (0.13 weight) is telling. In BTC's 16-year sample, trend-following bots got chopped up in 2013–2017's whipsaw and dumped in 2022's bear. The GA's signal was: don't trust trends, ride short bursts.

## What v1 tells us

1. **Sortino×survival optimizes for consistency, not returns.** Buy-hold has effectively infinite Sortino on BTC's 16yr sample; bot's 10.65 is dominated. Fitness function needs rethinking (per Vega's handoff question).
2. **Max DD 66.6% blows past the 40% ceiling** in MISSION.md success criteria. Deployment-blocked.
3. **Tight 2% stops + 100% position = single-bad-fill risk.** In a 2020-03 or 2022-05 gap, a 2% stop turns into 20%+ realized loss. v1's simulation doesn't model gap risk.
4. **1011 trades × 50 bps roundtrip = ~505% total cost drag.** Half the theoretical alpha eaten by fees. Realistic real-world costs would be worse (funding, slippage).
5. **No regime awareness.** Bot uses the same rules in 2013's exponential blowoff as in 2022's grinding bear. This is exactly what world-model enrichment should fix.

## Next steps (v2 design)

Per the handoff, v2 should:

1. **Add event/news awareness** — bot should see recent news events, macro state
2. **Expand genome** — add news-reactivity, macro-reactivity genes
3. **Consider alternative fitness** — Sharpe or Calmar; multi-objective (Sortino + DD + returns)
4. **Fix DD** — 40% max DD ceiling as hard constraint in fitness (fitness = 0 if DD > 40%?)
5. **Track cross-version convergence** — does v2's evolved genome resemble v1's? If yes → robust. If no → v1 was overfit to specific chart events.

## What NOT to do next

- Do not drop the trend-following signal just because v1 gave it 0.13 weight — that might be an artifact of BTC's specific chop periods
- Do not tune fitness to beat buy-hold on total returns — the 40× parabolic 2010→2013 is unrepeatable
- Do not skip OOS validation on v2 — hold out 2024–2026 as strict validation set (per success criterion #6)
